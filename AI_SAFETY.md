# AI Safety Contract

MindWell uses AI for supportive wellbeing conversation and non-clinical reflection only.

## Required language

Use wellbeing signals, stress signals, emotional patterns, risk indicators, trends, and supportive next steps. Do not state or imply a diagnosis, prescription, dosage, clinical determination, or emergency disposition.

## Crisis handling

Concerning language must remain outside premium flows. Immediate danger should receive a clear recommendation to contact local emergency services or a trusted person and connect with a qualified professional. The current product does not fabricate jurisdiction-specific emergency numbers.

## Current implementation

- The chat system instruction contains the non-diagnostic and crisis boundary.
- The dashboard labels reflections and progress as non-diagnostic.
- A deterministic safety gateway, versioned policy registry, multilingual golden set, output classifier, abstention metadata, and incident workflow remain to be implemented.

## Release rule

No model, prompt, provider, or safety-policy change should be described as production-safe until regression, multilingual, authorization, privacy, and failure-mode checks exist.
